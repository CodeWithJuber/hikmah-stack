# Decision & Strategy Frameworks

## The Complete Problem Classification Matrix

| Class | Signature | Tool | Example |
|-------|-----------|------|---------|
| **Clear** | Known cause, known fix | Apply the standard operating procedure | Typo in production → deploy the fix |
| **Complicated** | Unknown cause, knowable fix | Expert analysis or deep study | Performance regression → profile, instrument, diagnose |
| **Complex** | Emergent behavior, unknowable in advance | Small probes, watch what happens | Market entry → pilot in one city first |
| **Chaotic** | No patterns, system breaking | Stabilize first, then reassess | Production down → stop the bleeding, then diagnose |

**Rule**: Most problem-solving failures are category errors. Complicated surgery on a complex situation, or a complex situation forced into a checklist.

## The Consultation Protocol

1. **Before** you reveal your position, gather input
2. Invite at least one person likely to **disagree**
3. Set a consultation window with an **end date**
4. Close the window **publicly**
5. Decide and convert the decision into **ownership**
6. Ban re-litigation without **new evidence**

## The Risk Diversification Checklist

Single Points of Failure to Map:
- [ ] One client providing >30% revenue
- [ ] One supplier for a critical input
- [ ] One key person with undocumented knowledge
- [ ] One income stream
- [ ] One geographic location
- [ ] One technology stack with no migration path
- [ ] One backup system never tested

For Each:
- Cap the downside before counting the upside
- Rehearse the loss of any one gate — the plan should hurt, not kill
- After hedging, stop worrying; anxiety about the uncontrollable adds zero security

## The Pressure Protocol

### When Anxiety Spikes:
1. Write two columns: **Fear** (future) and **Grief** (past)
2. Cross both out
3. Write one line: the right thing to do **in the next hour**
4. Do that thing

### Before High-Stakes Moments:
1. **Capacity**: Regulate your inner state first
2. **Process**: Ease the task — simplify, sequence, remove friction
3. **Voice**: Untie the communication — prepare the plain sentence

### During Sustained Pressure:
- Channel 1 (Internal): Strategic patience — hold the line without panic-decisions
- Channel 2 (External): Daily reset — ten minutes of silence, reflection, or counsel
- Both channels must stay open. Grit alone burns out; connection alone drifts.

### After Hard Effort:
**Finish → Rise → Aim** (within 24 hours)

## The Recovery Loop

```
Error occurs
    ↓
Notice (don't deny)
    ↓
Own it in the first hour (before anyone asks)
    ↓
Name the fix ("my fault, and here is what changes")
    ↓
Stop persisting (persistence is the expensive part)
    ↓
Convert (acknowledge → redirect → produce good work)
    ↓
Recommission (end with restored mission, not reduced identity)
```

**Key metric**: Recovery latency, not error rate. Everyone errs; professionals cycle the loop fast.

## The Success Metabolism Protocol

After every win:
1. **Attribution Audit**: List what you controlled vs. what you rode
2. **Gratitude Exam**: Credit sources, convert gain into giving
3. **Self-Audit**: What did we get wrong that the win is hiding?
4. **Equilibrium**: Cap the celebration spike — neither exult nor despair
5. **Credit Transfer**: "This was willed, not willed by me" — before the story hardens

## The Communication Hierarchy

| Situation | Register | Rule |
|-----------|----------|------|
| Teaching | Wisdom first | Right content, right audience, right time |
| Persuading | Beautiful delivery | Tone that informs without humiliating |
| Debating | Better argument | Upgrade reasoning, not volume |
| Under hostility | Gentle speech | The harder the stakeholder, the softer the register |
| With provocateurs | "Peace" | Refuse to engage on their terms; starve the troll |
| All speech | Straight | Direct, checkable, no weaponized ambiguity |

## The Leadership Operating System

1. **Tone**: Gentleness retains; harshness is an attrition tax
2. **Process**: Structured consultation before the call
3. **Commitment**: After deciding, stop deliberating
4. **Trust**: Calibrated trust absorbs residual uncertainty
5. **Unity**: Aligned rows beat brilliant individuals
6. **Stewardship**: Every role is a loan with an audit date
7. **Hiring**: Two axes — capability + integrity — scored independently
8. **Building**: Mobilize resources, make beneficiaries co-builders, sequence stages, credit upward
