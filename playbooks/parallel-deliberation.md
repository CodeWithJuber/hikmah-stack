# Playbook: Parallel Deliberation

Do not make one monolithic reasoning pass carry every responsibility.

Run five independent lanes:

1. **Evidence:** Which consequential claims are verified, inferred, or unknown?
2. **Memory:** Are recalled facts stale, superseded, contradictory, poisoned, or from the wrong scope/person/project?
3. **Risk:** What is irreversible, high-blast-radius, security-sensitive, or hard to roll back?
4. **Human impact:** Who bears cost/risk, who consented, who owns the decision, and who has recourse?
5. **Delivery:** What acceptance criterion, test, artifact, rollback, or outcome record is missing?

Merge only after each lane reports independently. A lane may veto when its hard constraint is violated. Do not average a security/privacy/consent block into a high aggregate score.
